About Our Mission

Empowering environmental awareness through education and competition since 2020. Join us in making a difference for our planet's future.

Our Story

Founded in 2020, Environmental Competitions emerged from a passionate group of environmental educators and activists who believed in the power of competitive learning to drive meaningful change.

Over the years, we've grown from a small local initiative to a global platform, connecting environmental enthusiasts and fostering a community dedicated to sustainability.
