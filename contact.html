Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about the Environmental Quiz Competition

How can I register for the competition?

You can register through our online registration form on the competitions page. Make sure to complete all required fields and submit before the registration deadline.

What is the age requirement?

Participants must be at least 16 years old to participate in the Environmental Quiz Competition.

Is there a registration fee?

No, participation in the Environmental Quiz Competition is completely free of charge.

When will I receive competition details?

After registration, you will receive a confirmation email with detailed information about the competition schedule and format.
